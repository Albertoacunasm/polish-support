Support

A moment to bring out the shine. A 3D metal-polishing experience for iPhone.

Contact

fobicos.profeta6t@icloud.com

For app, purchase or privacy questions, include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Do not send passwords or card details.

Rotate and zoom

Drag with two fingers to see other sides; pinch with two fingers to zoom. Slide one finger over the metal to polish.

Leave and resume

Tap empty background to reveal the back arrow and return to the same object in the selector. Polish on the title screen resumes saved unfinished work.

Sound

Check the independent volumes in Settings and the iPhone silent setting. Ambience depends on the scene. The menu does not play demonstration audio.

Reinstalling and purchases

Progress is stored locally and deleting the app may remove it. In a version with the one-time unlock, use Restore purchases in Settings with the purchasing Apple Account. A connection may be required. Restoring a purchase does not itself restore progress. The open beta does not show purchases.

Accessibility

Help is available in Settings. Reduce Motion disables automatic turning. Object accessibility actions provide polishing bands, rotation, tilt and zoom.
