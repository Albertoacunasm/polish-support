Privacy

Prepared October 26, 2026.

Responsibility and contact

The developer of POLISH handles support and privacy requests at fobicos.profeta6t@icloud.com.

App data

POLISH stores polishing progress, completed objects, orientation and preferences locally. It does not require its own account or include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. Progress is not sent to a developer-operated game server.

Purchases

In a version with the one-time unlock, Apple processes payment. POLISH verifies access using StoreKit and does not receive your card details. Restoring access may require a connection and the purchasing Apple Account; it does not restore local progress. The open test beta does not show purchases.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains while available in the app. iOS or iCloud backups and restores depend on your settings; there is no developer-operated progress synchronization. Deleting the app may delete its data. Offloading while keeping documents and data is different. Reinstalling without a suitable backup does not guarantee recovery.

Diagnostics and support

Apple may handle crash reports and TestFlight feedback according to its services and your choices. If you contact support, the developer receives what you send and a reply address, uses it to handle your request, and keeps it only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. You may request deletion through the same contact. We cannot remotely recover progress that exists only on your device.

This website

This is a static site without analytics scripts, advertising or forms. GitHub Pages processes technical information, including IP addresses for security. The contact email uses Apple’s email service and forwarding. Those providers’ policies also apply.

Changes and requests

Relevant changes will be reflected here and in store declarations. Direct questions or requests to the contact above. Do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary sensitive information.
